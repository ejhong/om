# The OM Project

Om in four voices: a pitch trajectory you can watch and hear.

- **Listen:** [ejhong.github.io/om](https://ejhong.github.io/om/om_prj.html)
- **Position paper:** *The construction of OM: voice, breath and the scaffolding of meditation* (Eugene Jhong, 2026): [read online](https://ejhong.github.io/om/om_paper.html) · [PDF](https://ejhong.github.io/om/om_paper.pdf)

The paper proposes that OM is a constructed sound: a fast sequence of short notes in eight sections, sung in four types. It also proposes that OM's breath, inverted, is the scaffolding of meditation and breathwork. It describes; it does not prescribe.

Objections and corrections are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/ejhong/om/issues).
